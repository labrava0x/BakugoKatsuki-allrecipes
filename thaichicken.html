Spicy Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Krapow Gai)

Source: Allrecipes Original Recipe

Why This Recipe Isn't Garbage. One of the recipes from allrecipes worth eating, and it is spicy

Most recipes on the internet are weak and watered down. This Pad Krapow Gai actually uses high heat, bird's eye chilies, and garlic to give it a sharp, aggressive kick instead of wasting time with mild nonsense.

Key Flavor Drivers

  • Fresh Thai bird's eye chilies (don't tone them down)
  • High-heat stir-fry technique
  • Savory, rich soy and oyster sauce base
